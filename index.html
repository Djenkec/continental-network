How it works

From application to partner in five steps

  1. Apply

    Send us your channel link and a few details through the form below.

  2. Review

    We check the channel's content, history and policy status.

  3. Agreement

    You get a clear contract with the revenue split and terms in writing.

  4. Connect

    You accept the invitation in YouTube Studio to link your channel to our network.

  5. Grow

    We start protecting, reporting and working on growth with you.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I keep ownership of my channel?

Yes. The channel and your content stay yours. Joining the network links the channel for monetization and rights management only.

How is revenue shared?

The split is set out in your agreement before you join, so you know exactly what you receive. Every month you get a statement showing the figures.

Can I leave the network?

Yes. The agreement states the notice period and how the channel is unlinked.

How long does the review take?

We reply to every application. Response times are confirmed in our first message to you.
