Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Who we are

Continental Network is a YouTube multi-channel network. For questions about your data, write to info@continentalnetwork.org.

What we collect

When you apply through the form on this site we receive: your name, email address, channel link, content category, country and any message you write. We don't use advertising cookies or tracking on this site.

Why we use it

Only to review your application and reply to you. The legal basis is your consent, which you give by ticking the box on the form, and the steps needed before a possible agreement with you.

Who processes it

Applications are delivered to our team by email through the form service Web3Forms (web3forms.com). The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. We don't sell or share your data with anyone else.

How long we keep it

If we don't start working together, we delete your application within 12 months. If we do, your details become part of our agreement and are kept as long as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent, at any time by writing to info@continentalnetwork.org. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Politika privatnosti

Zadnja izmjena: 3. novembar 2026.

Ko smo mi

Continental Network je YouTube mreža kanala (MCN). Za pitanja o tvojim podacima piši na info@continentalnetwork.org.

Šta prikupljamo

Kad se prijaviš preko forme na ovom sajtu, dobijamo: ime, email adresu, link kanala, kategoriju sadržaja, državu i poruku koju napišeš. Na sajtu ne koristimo reklamne kolačiće niti praćenje.

Zašto ih koristimo

Samo da pregledamo tvoju prijavu i javimo ti se. Pravni osnov je tvoja saglasnost, koju daješ označavanjem kvadratića na formi, i koraci potrebni prije mogućeg ugovora s tobom.

Ko ih obrađuje

Prijave stižu našem timu emailom preko servisa Web3Forms (web3forms.com). Sajt je na GitHub Pages i učitava fontove s Google Fonts. Tvoje podatke ne prodajemo niti dijelimo s drugima.

Koliko ih čuvamo

Ako ne počnemo saradnju, prijavu brišemo u roku od 12 mjeseci. Ako počnemo, podaci postaju dio ugovora i čuvamo ih koliko zakon nalaže.

Tvoja prava

U svakom trenutku možeš tražiti uvid, ispravku ili brisanje podataka, ili povući saglasnost, pišući na info@continentalnetwork.org. Imaš i pravo žalbe nadležnom organu za zaštitu ličnih podataka.
